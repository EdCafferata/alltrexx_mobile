Who is responsible?

The IT Crowd (Ed Cafferata), based in the Netherlands, builds and runs this app.

What data?

Your track files (GPX) and the data you add yourself. No account is needed for the basics.

Where is it stored?

Your GPX data stays yours — on your device or wherever you keep it. No data is sold to third parties. See alltrexx.live for the web app.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we keep no accounts or personal data on our servers, we normally hold nothing about you. Still have a question about your data or your GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection)? Email us.
