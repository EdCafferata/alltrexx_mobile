1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Alltrexx Mobile is the companion app of the website https://alltrexx.live, a live map of moving things (aircraft, ships, trains, cyclists, hikers, cars). The app lets a person share the position of their own iPhone (near) live on that map and see themselves on it. Audience: hikers, cyclists, sailors, pilots and travellers who want friends/family to follow their trip, without a social network or account.

2. HOW TO USE / ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login or credentials are needed.
- First launch: short onboarding, then allow location access.
- Choose a tracker type (person, bike, car, boat, train, plane) and a display name. The app then requests a free anonymous tracker key from alltrexx.live (no e-mail, no password).
- Status tab: start/stop tracking; shows live status, last position, speed and the offline queue.
- Tracking map tab: your own track, with optional layers.
- Live map tab: opens alltrexx.live for your tracker type. Each tracker name there has a "Report name" button to flag objectionable content.
- Settings: optional personal map keys (OpenWeatherMap for precipitation, and a third-party aviation-airspace map service), a "Delete my data" button and an optional tip jar.
- "Delete my data" removes the tracker and all its stored positions on the server and wipes local data (Guideline 5.1.1(v)).
- Tip jar: three optional consumable In-App Purchases. They unlock nothing; the app is fully functional without them.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- alltrexx.live (our own backend): receives positions and shows the live map.
- Apple MapKit: base map. Open-Meteo: weather/wind. OpenStreetMap-based tile services: optional map layers.
- OpenWeatherMap and a third-party aviation-airspace map service: optional layers, only with a key the user enters.
- Apple StoreKit: optional tips.
No AI, third-party authentication, ads, or tracking/analytics SDKs.

4. USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND MODERATION
The only user-entered content is the tracker display name, shown next to the position on the public map at https://alltrexx.live. There is no messaging, chat, photos or other UGC. Moderation (Guideline 1.2):
- Filtering: objectionable names (profanity, hate speech, sexual terms, in English and Dutch) are rejected by the server at key creation (HTTP 400); the name is not accepted and the app shows an error.
- Reporting: every name on the live map has a "Report name" button, reachable in the app via the "Live map" tab, which sends a report to our server.
- Removal/blocking: at the report threshold a name is automatically masked ("Reported name"); an administrator reviews reports and can remove the tracker. Users can also delete their own tracker and data anytime via "Delete my data".
- Contact: an abuse-report address is published on the support page https://cafferata.info/alltrexx/.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions identically in all regions.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not a navigation or safety-of-life aid; map data comes from the open/public services above under their own licences.

7. GUIDELINE 5 (LEGAL) — NO AI / DEEP-SYNTHESIS
This app contains no generative-AI, deep-synthesis, chatbot or large-language-model functionality and is not connected to any such service — it is a GPS location-sharing app. A previous metadata string named a third-party aviation-airspace map-data provider; that name was removed from all metadata to prevent a false automated keyword match. The deep-synthesis/generative-AI regulations therefore do not apply.
