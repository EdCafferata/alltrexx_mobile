Alltrexx Mobile turns your phone into a live location tracker. Pick what you are — person, boat, bike, car, train or plane — and your position is shared live on alltrexx.live, so friends, family or your crew can follow you on the map in real time.

Free and simple
- No subscription and no account — just choose a name for your tracker and go
- Your position appears on alltrexx.live within seconds

Made for live following
- Near real-time updates, also while the app runs in the background
- A smart offline queue bridges gaps in coverage and catches up automatically
- Battery-friendly: GPS accuracy and update rate adapt to your speed, so standing still barely uses power
- Keeps tracking after a restart thanks to significant-location monitoring

Maps that match your journey
- Your own live map showing the route you have shared this session
- Category-specific layers, just like the alltrexx.live web map: sea marks for boats, cycling and hiking routes, railways for trains and an aviation layer for planes
- Optional precipitation (OpenWeatherMap) and airspace (OpenAIP) layers with a free key

Your data, your control
- Only your position and the name you choose are shared
- Delete your tracker and all local data from the app at any time
- No third-party tracking and no advertising

Tap the live button to jump straight to yourself on alltrexx.live.
